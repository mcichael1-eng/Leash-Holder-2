Read in order

Each chapter assumes only the ones before it. If you know nothing about computers, start at Chapter 1.

Look for the boxes

Green-edged boxes define terms. Gold boxes show what the leading labs do right now. Copper boxes correct common mistakes.

Do the exercises

Every chapter ends with exercises from Easy to Challenge. None of them need you to know how to code.

Use the glossary

Forgot a word? The glossary at the end lists every defined term and links back to where it is explained.
